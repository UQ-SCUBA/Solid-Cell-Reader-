
Custom PCB with
STM32G051F8P6 as a MCU
pin 1 PB7,8: usart1 RX
pin 20 PB3,4,5,6 : usart1 TX
pin 19 PA14,15 : sys_SWCLK
pin 18 PA13 : sys_SWDIO
Pin 9  PA0 : optional external wake pin (WKUP1), pulled high to wake
pin 10 PA1 : cell MOSFET switch, active low (pulled low = cell on)
pin 11 PA4 : DAC out

reading a FD02 cell from pyro science via mcu UART1

voltage divider on pin 11 uses R1==20k, R2==1k (was 33k/1k — changed to allow
higher output, ~2.6 bar pO2 equivalent)

No LEDs on this revision of the board.
